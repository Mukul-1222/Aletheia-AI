# FINAL25 research notes

FINAL25 is a transition release between the objective-value work of FINAL24 and the stronger from-scratch language learner planned next.

The main research questions are:

1. Does a learned tokenizer improve sample efficiency and effective context without importing external linguistic knowledge?
2. Do prediction-match and cross-source consistency provide useful continual-learning outcomes in open-world ingestion?
3. Does a learned objective-value model become predictive once it receives sufficiently many real transition outcomes?
4. Does improved language representation improve multimodal grounding, planning, and communication without adding hard-coded semantic rules?

Aletheia is still a research prototype. Neither general intelligence nor sentience is established by these mechanisms.
